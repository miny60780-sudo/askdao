万年历 · Almanac

Chinese Almanac

The traditional Chinese almanac (huánglì 黄历) lists, for every day, its lunar date, stem-branch (ganzhi) cycle, solar term and the activities folk custom considers favourable or best avoided. Pick a date to see its entry.

Data computed locally by the open-source library lunar-javascript (6tail, MIT licence). Terms without a reliable English equivalent are shown in Chinese with pinyin. Traditional folk reference, for entertainment only.
